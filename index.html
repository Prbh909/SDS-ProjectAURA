About SDS Project AURA

Advanced Unified Ride Assistance

CLASS PROJECT
OBJECTIVE

Demonstrate how school transport visibility, QR-assisted attendance and optional AI identity verification can be organized in one web platform.

TECHNOLOGY

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet maps, QR decoding, browser storage and GPS concepts.

AI COMPONENT

Planned computer-vision-based photo verification. It is not implemented or claimed as active in this prototype.

LIMITATIONS

GPS is simulated, data is browser-specific, and no real school or facial data is connected.
